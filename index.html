🏆 Accepted at ICSAE 2025 · Paper ID: ICSAE2025-11005 Master's Thesis Research
IIT (ISM) Dhanbad · 2024–2025

Machine Learning Approaches for Earthquake Declustering

Master's research establishing data-driven machine learning methodologies for earthquake catalogue declustering — disentangling triggered aftershocks and foreshock sequences from stationary Poissonian background seismicity to significantly advance seismic hazard assessment and probabilistic forecasting.

Spatiotemporal Cluster Separation
  • Catalogue Benchmark: Investigated high-density earthquake catalogues from the New Zealand GeoNet network across complex strike-slip and subduction zones.
  • Supervised Benchmarking: Implemented and compared XGBoost, Random Forest, Support Vector Machines (SVM), and Gradient Boosting classifiers against conventional deterministic windowing (Gardner-Knopoff, Reasenberg).
  • Unsupervised & Hybrid Ensembles: Developed unsupervised spatial clustering using Self-Organizing Maps (SOM), HDBSCAN, and a novel SOM+DBSCAN hybrid pipeline for adaptive spatiotemporal cluster boundary resolution.
  • Statistical Seismology Validation: Evaluated seismicity catalogue completeness (Mc), b-value spatial stability, and modified Omori law aftershock decay rates.
Python Scikit-learn XGBoost HDBSCAN MiniSom GeoNet API NumPy & Pandas Matplotlib & Seaborn Statistical Seismology

Outcome

  • 97.44% classification accuracy (XGBoost, on synthetic test data), with an ROC AUC of 0.98.
  • Catalogue separated into 230,758 background events (58.23%) and 165,509 triggered events (41.77%).

Full methodology, figures and per-model scores are on the earthquake declustering results page.

Petrophysical Case Study of Gorgonichthys-1 Well

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

March 2024 – April 2024

Comprehensive petrophysical evaluation of the Gorgonichthys-1 exploration well, focusing on sonic tool design, wireline log quality control, porosity derivation, velocity analysis, shale volume estimation, and hydrocarbon pay zone determination.

Read Full Summary & Methodology

Detailed Investigation Steps:

  • Wireline log QC: statistical sanity checks, environmental corrections, and outlier filtering.
  • Cross-plot analysis: density-neutron lithology identification and mineral volume estimation.
  • Acoustic velocity analysis: compressional and shear wave travel time calibration.
  • Shale volume determination: linear and non-linear (Larionov, Clavier) gamma-ray formulations.
  • Water saturation evaluation (Sw): Archie and Simandoux shaly-sand equations for net pay flags.

Tools: Python, Matplotlib, NumPy, LAS data processing
